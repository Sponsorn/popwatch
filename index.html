How it works

  1. Popwatch listens for the sharp crack of a kernel and counts pops over the last 3 seconds.
  2. Three test kernels go in first. When they pop, the oil is hot. Then 30 seconds off the heat with the rest of the corn, and back on.
  3. A batch rises fast, peaks, then trails off. Popwatch tracks the peak and tells you to pull the pot once the rate falls under 15% of it.
  4. Stragglers cost you nothing. Scorch costs you the batch. Audio never leaves your phone.
